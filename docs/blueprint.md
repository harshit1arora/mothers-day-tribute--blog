# **App Name**: Mother's Day Tribute Blog

## Core Features:

- Home Page UI: Implement the Home Page with Hero Section (Carousel), Explore by Category Section, and Recent Articles Section as per the design.
- Story Detail Page: Create dynamic Story Detail Pages (/articles/:id) to display the full article content with title, author, date, and category.
- Search Functionality: Implement a client-side search bar that filters articles by Title or Excerpt from the local JSON data.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Soft Lavender (#E6E6FA) for a gentle and calming feel.
- Secondary color: Warm Beige (#F5F5DC) to provide a sense of comfort and support.
- Accent: Dusty Rose (#D8BFD8) for highlights and call-to-action elements, evoking feelings of love and appreciation.
- Clean and readable typography for article content and headings.
- Simple, elegant icons for categories and navigation.
- Clean and spacious layout to improve readability and focus on content.
